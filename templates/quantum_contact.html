Frequently Asked Questions

How fast do inter-account transfers settle?

Internal transfers between your QuantumLeap accounts are settled instantly in real time.

Are my investment balances insured?

Securities are protected up to $500,000 via SIPC, and cash balances in High-Yield Cash are FDIC insured up to $2,500,000 through program banks.

How do I view my detailed transactions?

Visit your Dashboard and click "View Transactions" on any specific account card.
